# Verification report authoring card

A report presents recorded calculations and the interpretation they support.
State whether it is the manual manuscript analysis or a secondary MCP/local
check. Protocol cards are documented guidance, not proof of a historical run.

Include:

1. The question and the scope of the interpretation.
2. Inputs, snapshot provenance, cell and animal counts, and missing metadata.
3. Expression and age-contrast tables with the actual formula/scale.
4. Cluster or CCI summaries, selection rules, and filters where applicable.
5. Executed stages and links to the corresponding outputs.
6. Direction/significance comparisons with distinct estimands identified.
7. Negative findings, disagreements, uncertainties, and untested mechanisms.
8. Source records, scripts, and a statement of whether a new run occurred.

Every number must be traceable to a named result or historical reference.
The comparison labels in [the protocol](../aging_atlas_evaluation_protocol.md)
do not assert numerical reproduction. Preserve the old labels separately when
correcting interpretation; do not alter numerical results to fit the narrative.
Qualitative targets are not measurements. Do not replace a P value rounded to
zero with an invented exact value.

A discrepancy's cause may be unknown. Do not state that seed, package version,
or subsampling caused it unless tested. Missing significance does not establish
absence, and a cell-level pattern does not replace mouse-level inference.
